What does a block cost?

D1-03 · sections 3-5 All labs

Four ways to turn C_in x H x W into C_out x H x W (stride 1, same padding). Each box is a conv layer with its weight count; the bars add them up. Which numbers move when you change the widths, k or H?

the four blocks chips = tensor shapes (C x H x W), boxes = conv layers

cost

Try this - predict first, then check

  1. C_in = C_out = 512: switch k from 3 to 7. How much do the standard and the depthwise-sep. blocks grow?
    why

    The standard conv has k²·C_in·C_out weights, so it grows by 49/9 = 5.4x. The depthwise-separable block is k²·C_in + C_in·C_out: only the small depthwise part grows, the 1x1 (C_in·C_out) is unchanged, so it grows by about 1.1x. Large kernels are almost free in a depthwise conv - that is why ConvNeXt uses 7x7 depthwise.

  2. Slide C_in = C_out from 32 to 1024 (k = 3). Does the "depthwise-sep. saves" factor keep growing?
    why

    The ratio is 1 / (1/C_out + 1/k²): 7x at 32 channels, 8.7x at 256, and it approaches k² = 9 but never passes it. At large widths almost all of the remaining cost is the 1x1 pointwise conv (the orange segment).

  3. Switch the bars to log, then double H = W. Which bars move, and by how much?
    why

    Parameters do not move at all: a conv's weights do not depend on the image size. Every MACs bar moves right by the same factor 4: each weight is used once per output pixel, so MACs = weights x H x W. Halving the resolution (stride 2, pooling) is the cheapest way to cut compute.

  4. Keep C_in = 256 and set C_out = 512. What appears in the bottleneck, and how big is it?
    why

    When the shape changes, the shortcut cannot be the identity: it becomes a 1x1 projection with C_in·C_out = 131 072 weights - more than the three convs of the block together (86 016). ResNets therefore project only in the first block of each stage; all the others keep the identity shortcut for free.

  5. At 256 channels depthwise-separable needs 8.7x fewer MACs. Is it 8.7x faster on a GPU?
    answer

    Usually not. A depthwise conv does very little arithmetic per byte it reads, so it is limited by memory bandwidth, not by multiply-adds; MobileNet / EfficientNet are much less fast on a GPU than their MAC count suggests. MACs estimate compute; only timing on the target hardware measures latency (notebook section 6, D2-04).
