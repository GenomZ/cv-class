  1. Move the score threshold from 0.30 to 0.90. Which numbers change: precision, recall, F1, AP?
        
    why
    Only P, R and F1: the threshold picks one point on the curve. AP is computed from the whole ranked list,
          so it summarises every threshold at once and does not depend on the one you deploy. (That is also why detectors are
          evaluated with a very low score threshold, 0.001-0.05: cutting the list early would cut the curve and lose AP.)
    2. 
      
  2. #3 is a duplicate (a FP). Predict how much AP gains if its score drops below every other detection. Drag its bar to the left end.
          Then put it back and drag the background FP #5 to the top instead.
        
    answer
    All-points AP goes from 0.830 to 0.917 with the duplicate ranked last, and drops to 0.626 with #5 on top.
          Only the order matters: a FP above the TPs lowers the precision of every point after it, a FP below the last TP costs nothing.
          AP rewards a detector whose confidence is honest.
    3. 
      
  3. The image really contains 6 objects, not 4: set #GT to 6. What happens to the curve and to AP? Can any threshold fix it?
        
    why
    Recall can never exceed TP / #GT = 4/6: the curve ends at R = 0.67 and the area to its right is lost (AP 0.830 → 0.554).
          A missed object is not in the ranked list at all, so no threshold and no re-ranking brings it back; only a better detector does.
    4. 
      
  4. Flip #1 (score 0.95) from TP to FP. Flip it back and flip #7 (score 0.43) instead. Both are the same mistake, one object missed
        and one more FP. Which costs more AP?
        
    answer
    #1: AP 0.830 → 0.357; #7: 0.830 → 0.688. A FP at the top lowers the precision of every point after it,
          a miss at the bottom only cuts the end of the curve. Errors made with high confidence dominate AP.
    5. 
      
  5. Switch the sample points to 11, then 101. Why do the three AP values differ slightly, and which one does boxes.average_precision return by default?
        
    why
    All three integrate the same envelope: exactly (all points, VOC2010+, your Exercise 5), at 11 recall levels
          (VOC2007) or at 101 (COCO). Sampling over- or under-counts the steps a little. The default method="interp101" is COCO's;
          Exercise 5 is the all-points version.
