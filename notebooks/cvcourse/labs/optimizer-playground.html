Try this - predict first, then check

  1. On the ravine SGD zig-zags at η = 0.9. Predict η = 1.1, then drag there. Which optimiser takes the largest η before it blows up?
    why

    SGD multiplies the across-ravine coordinate by (1 − 2η) per step; for η > 1 every bounce grows and the loss explodes. Momentum survives up to 2η < 2(1 + μ). AdamW never explodes (each coordinate moves about η per step) but at large η it rattles around the minimum. No single SGD η is fast along the ravine and stable across it: that is ill-conditioning.

  2. Set μ = 0: orange becomes plain SGD with η = 0.09 and crawls. Back to μ = 0.9: why no zig-zag, although it now moves 10x faster along the ravine?
    why

    The velocity b sums the gradients. Across the ravine they flip sign at every crossing and cancel; along it they always agree and add up to about 1 / (1 − μ) = 10 steps. You get a smooth, slightly overshooting swing. At μ = 0.99 the overshoot dominates and the path circles for a long time.

  3. Inspect step 1 (click the loss chart near t = 1). The gradient is 8x larger across the ravine, yet AdamW moves both coordinates equally. Why?
    why

    At t = 1, m̂ = g and √v̂ = |g|, so Δw = −η sign(g): exactly η per coordinate, whatever the gradient's size. Adam rescales every coordinate by its own recent gradient magnitude; that is why its path starts at 45° and why Adam's η is roughly a step size in weight units.

  4. Switch to the saddle (start just off the ridge w1 = 0). Who escapes first, and why does SGD hesitate?
    why

    Near the ridge the w1-gradient is tiny (about −w1), so SGD's step η g is tiny and it first drifts towards the saddle point. AdamW divides the tiny gradient by its tiny running RMS and steps away at about η per step. Plateaus and saddles are common in real networks. Start exactly on w1 = 0 and nobody escapes: there is no w1 gradient at all.

  5. On the curved valley, raise momentum's η from 0.003 to 0.03. Where does it go wrong?
    why

    The valley walls get steeper as |w0| grows (curvature across the valley about 20(1 + 4 w0²)). η = 0.03 is stable near w0 = 0 but not on the steep parts; the velocity carries the path past the minimum onto the steep wall, the bounces grow, and it is thrown out after about 20 steps. The safe η is set by the sharpest region the path visits, and momentum makes it visit more.
