presets

Try this - predict first, then check

  1. With the default network (128 px, depth 4, base 16), which level holds most of the ~1.9 M parameters, and which level holds most of the activation memory?
    why

    Parameters of a 3x3 conv grow with channels squared (9 cin cout), and channels double per level: the bottleneck alone holds ~46% and the two deepest levels almost 90%. Activations grow with height x width, which shrinks 4x per level: level 0 (full resolution, few channels) alone holds about half of the memory, levels 0-1 about 80%. Training memory is dominated by the top of the U, the model size by the bottom.

  2. Set the input to 100. Before looking: where exactly does it break, and why does 128 work?
    why

    Max-pooling floors: 100 → 50 → 25 → 12 → 6. On the way up, 6 → 12 matches, but 12 → 24 meets a 25 x 25 skip and torch.cat fails ("Expected size 24 but got size 25"). The side must be divisible by 2depth = 16. Fixes: pad the input to 112 (and crop the output back), resize to 96 or 112, or crop / pad the skip inside the decoder. 130 fails one level higher (64 vs 65).

  3. Keep 100 px and switch the skips off. Does it run now? Is the result usable?
    why

    It runs (nothing is concatenated), but the output is 96 x 96 while the mask is 100 x 100: the loss fails instead, or a resize silently shifts the boundaries. Turning the skips off also removes about 10% of the parameters (the decoder convs see half the channels).

  4. Double the base channels (16 → 32). Predict the factor for parameters and for activation memory; then add one level of depth.
    why

    Parameters scale with channels squared: about 4x. Activations scale with channels: about 2x. One more level adds a new bottleneck with 4x the parameters of the old one (the total roughly quadruples) but almost no activation memory, because it sits at 1/32 of the resolution.

  5. Click original 2015 (valid convolutions, 572 px input, 64 base channels). Why is the output only 388 x 388, and why are the skips cropped?
    why

    Unpadded 3x3 convs lose 2 px each, 4 per block, at every level on the way down and up. The decoder map is therefore smaller than its skip, so the skip is centre-cropped before the concat. The paper predicts the 388 px centre of a 572 px tile and tiles a big image with overlapping inputs (the overlap-tile strategy, section 6). Same padding keeps input = output and made this bookkeeping disappear.
