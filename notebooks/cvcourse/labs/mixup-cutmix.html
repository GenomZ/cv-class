Mixup and CutMix

D1-02 · section 3 All labs

Both build one training image from two, and a soft label from their labels with the same weight λ. Mixup blends every pixel; CutMix pastes a box of image j into image i and sets λ to the area left. Drag the box, move λ, or sample λ the way training does.

Try this - predict first, then check

  1. CutMix with λ = 0.75 on this 128 px image: is the pasted square 32 px or 64 px wide?
    answer

    64 px. λ is the share of image i that is kept, so the box covers 1 − λ = 25 % of the area, and a square with a quarter of the area has half the side: √0.25 * 128 = 64. The label is 0.75 person, 0.25 cat.

  2. Drag the CutMix box until half of it hangs outside the image. Does the label change? Should it?
    why

    Yes. Like the paper, v2.CutMix clips the box to the image and recomputes λ = 1 − (clipped box area) / (H * W), so the label always matches the pixels that were really pasted. The sampled λ (slider) and the λ used for the label differ whenever the box is clipped.

  3. Set α = 0.2 and press +200 samples. How often is λ a real blend, between 0.3 and 0.7? Then try α = 1 and α = 4.
    answer

    About 13 % at α = 0.2 (two thirds of the draws are below 0.1 or above 0.9: almost clean images), 40 % at α = 1 (uniform), 75 % at α = 4. Small α is a gentle regulariser and the usual choice for Mixup (0.2 - 0.4); CutMix uses α = 1.

  4. Mixup with λ = 0.5: what is the best prediction for this image, and how low can its cross-entropy go?
    why

    Predicting [0.5, 0.5] exactly; the loss is then the entropy of the target, ln 2 = 0.69, never 0. Soft targets put a floor under the training loss and punish confident predictions, the same effect as label smoothing in section 4. The training loss of a Mixup run is therefore not comparable with a plain run.
