Interactive labs

26 labs · offline · no install

One mechanism per page. Predict what will happen, then drag, paint and slide to check. Every lab is also embedded in its notebook with labs.show("name").

Day 1 · ConvNets: from pixels to transfer learning
D1-01Convolution playgroundEdit a 3x3 kernel and watch the output; hover any pixel to see patch x kernel = one number. Stride, padding, dilation. D1-01Receptive field explorerStack conv / pool / dilated layers, click any unit and see exactly which input pixels it depends on; r and jump per layer with the recursion filled in. D1-02Learning-rate schedule designerConstant, step decay, warm-up + cosine and torch's OneCycleLR side by side; drag the cursor to read the LR at any step with the formula filled in. D1-02Mixup and CutMixBlend or cut-and-paste two images and watch the soft label follow; drag the CutMix box, sample lambda ~ Beta(alpha, alpha) and see what alpha does. D1-02Optimizer playgroundSGD, SGD + momentum and AdamW race on 2-D loss surfaces: drag the start, change the learning rates and momentum, inspect every update. D1-03What does a block cost?Same input, four blocks: standard conv, ResNet bottleneck, depthwise-separable, Inception-style 1x1 then kxk. Parameters and multiply-adds with the formulas filled in. D1-03Gradient flow: plain vs residualA real forward and backward pass through a deep random ReLU network: per-layer gradient norms, plain vs identity skip connections, with or without BatchNorm, three initialisation scales. D1-04Freeze, unfreeze, discriminative LRsResNet-50 by stage with its true parameter counts: click to freeze / unfreeze, set base LR and decay, and read off trainable parameters, per-group learning rates and the transfer regime. D1-04Grad-CAM by handGrad-CAM as a visible weighted sum: eight feature maps of the photo, one weight alpha_k each, the sum, the ReLU and the upsampled heat map; presets play the pooled gradients of three classes.
Day 2 · Object detection
D2-01IoU playgroundDrag and resize a prediction against a ground-truth box: intersection, union, enclosing box, IoU / GIoU / DIoU / CIoU live, gradient arrows, and the loss curves that show why 1 - IoU is flat once the boxes stop overlapping. D2-01NMS playgroundStep through greedy NMS, linear / Gaussian Soft-NMS and class-wise NMS on scored candidate boxes; drag boxes, move the IoU and score thresholds, and see when NMS deletes a real object. D2-01PR curve and APRe-rank the notebook's 8 detections, flip TP / FP, change the number of objects and the score threshold: the precision-recall curve, its envelope, AP (all-points, 11-point, 101-point) and P / R / F1 update live. D2-02Anchor matchingDrag ground-truth boxes over a grid of anchors and watch the IoU rule label each anchor positive, ignored or negative. Stride, scales, ratios, thresholds, low-quality matches. D2-02Box encodingDrag an anchor and a ground-truth box and read the regression target (tx, ty, tw, th); set predicted deltas and watch the decoded box; smooth-L1 vs L1 vs L2. D2-02RoI pooling vs RoIAlignDrag a region of interest over an 8x8 feature map: RoI pooling rounds the box and its bins, RoIAlign samples bilinearly at exact positions. Hover a sample to see its 4 neighbours and weights. D2-03Focal lossCE vs focal loss as a function of p_t, then the population view: who contributes the total loss (or gradient) when 100 000 easy negatives meet 20 hard positives. D2-03FPN levelsResize one object and see which pyramid level P2-P7 gets it under the FPN RoI rule, RetinaNet anchor matching and FCOS ranges; plus the top-down pathway with live tensor shapes. D2-03Grid assignmentDrag shapes over a G x G grid: the cell holding each box centre is responsible. Watch the (G, G, 5 + C) target tensor, collisions, and how rare positive cells are. D2-04Hungarian matchingDrag DETR's object queries over the ground-truth boxes: the class + L1 + GIoU cost matrix, the optimal one-to-one matching, the 'no object' queries, and why greedy matching is not enough.
Day 3 · Segmentation and the frontier
D3-01Segmentation metricsPaint a prediction mask over a ground truth and watch TP / FP / FN / TN, the confusion matrix, pixel accuracy, precision, recall, IoU and Dice move. D3-01Transposed convolutionStep through a transposed convolution: every input value stamps a scaled copy of the kernel into the output. Count the overlaps and watch the checkerboard appear; compare with resize + conv. D3-02Sliding-window inferenceTile a big image into overlapping windows: see the tiles, how many cover each pixel, the forward-pass cost, and how overlap and Gaussian blending hide the weak predictions near tile borders. D3-02U-Net shapesThe U-Net drawn with the tensor shape of every block: change input size, depth, width, skips and upsampling, see where parameters and activation memory live, and where an awkward input size breaks the concat. D3-03Mask paste-backMask R-CNN stretches one fixed 28x28 soft mask over every box: drag and resize the box, change the mask resolution and the threshold, hover a pixel to see its 4 bilinear weights. D3-04CLIP zero-shot geometryZero-shot classification as geometry: drag an image embedding and the text-prompt embeddings around a circle; cosine -> logit scale -> softmax, normalisation, prompt ensembling, add a class. D3-04Patches and attentionCut the photo into p x p patches (tokens), then click a patch: softmax(q.k / sqrt(d)) over all patches, temperature, position encoding, and shuffling the patches.
