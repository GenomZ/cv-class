  1. Street, greedy NMS: press step until done. Then predict what goes wrong at IoU threshold 0.30, and at 0.70.
        
    why
    At 0.30 the best box on person A also removes every box on person B (A and B overlap with IoU 0.43): a real
          object is deleted. At 0.70 some duplicates overlap their kept box less than that and survive as false positives. 0.40-0.55 is right;
          the one false positive left (#12) is a background detection, which NMS cannot remove: raise the score threshold above 0.34 instead.

  2. Switch to crowd: the notebook's four boxes, people A and B overlap with IoU 0.61. Find an IoU threshold that keeps both people
        and no duplicate. Is it still right for the street scene?
        
    why
    0.60-0.80 works here, but at 0.60 the street scene already keeps a horse duplicate (IoU 0.58 with the kept horse box).
          Greedy NMS uses one threshold for every image, and crowded images need a different one.

  3. Stay on crowd, IoU threshold back to 0.50, and switch to Soft-NMS Gaussian. Step through and watch the "now" column.
        Why does person B survive while the duplicates do not?
        
    why
    Each kept box decays the others by exp(−IoU²/σ). B's best box overlaps A's with IoU 0.58, so its score only halves
          (0.90 → 0.46); A's duplicate overlaps with 0.85 and drops to 0.19, under the score threshold. Set the score threshold to 0 and nothing is
          removed, only re-ranked: 0.95, 0.46, 0.08, 0.02, the notebook's Soft-NMS figure (#1-#4 here are #0-#3 there).
    4. 
      
  4. Scene person + horse, greedy NMS, class-agnostic. Where did the horse go? Now switch to class-wise.
        
    why
    The person stands in front of the horse and their boxes overlap with IoU ≈ 0.6, so class-agnostic NMS treats the horse
          as a duplicate of the person. Class-wise NMS (torchvision's batched_nms) only compares boxes of the same class.

  5. Drag a duplicate away from its object until it is no longer removed. Does it then count as a found object?
        
    why
    It survives once its IoU with every kept box is below the threshold, and becomes a false positive: NMS removes
          overlaps, it does not judge quality. "New random scene" gives more cases to predict.
